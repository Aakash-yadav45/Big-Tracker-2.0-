# **BigTracker – Field Employee Tracking & Training Management**

## **Project Overview**
BigTracker is a mobile application designed to monitor and manage field employees who visit clients to resolve queries and provide training for **Healthray ERP**. The app ensures transparency and accountability by tracking the number of visits employees make daily, recording their conversations, and maintaining a log of their activities.

Employees must **check in at a designated time**, **record their journey**, **take a selfie upon arrival**, **log their conversations**, **summarize discussions**, and **sync session data to the server**. This ensures a structured workflow and provides admins with real-time insights into employee performance.

Admins can **add new employees**, **view all employees**, and **track session details**, helping organizations streamline field operations efficiently.

---

## **Objectives**

### **Client-Side (Employee) Objectives**
- **Ensure accurate tracking of field visits** with real-time check-in and check-out.
- **Record visit details**, including location, client name, and visit summary.
- **Enable secure and structured session logging**, including:
  - Selfie verification upon arrival.
  - Full conversation recording for reference.
  - Short text summary of discussions.
- **Ensure seamless session synchronization** by uploading data to the server.
- **Monitor daily visits** to maintain accountability and productivity.

### **Admin-Side Objectives**
- **Add new employees** to the system and manage their accounts.
- **View and track employee visits** in real time.
- **Access session details**, including location, recordings, summaries, and timestamps.
- **Monitor employee activity** to ensure compliance with assigned tasks.
- **Generate reports (future scope)** to analyze employee performance trends.
